# 15–20 Minute Technical Demonstration Guide

1. (2 min) Explain business goal, personas, six-course KB, and architecture.
2. (3 min) Show Retell agent prompt, voice choice, dynamic variables, and state flow.
3. (3 min) Demonstrate identity check, permission, one-question-at-a-time discovery, and course recommendation.
4. (2 min) Ask about fees and placement guarantee; show factual escalation and refusal to guarantee jobs.
5. (2 min) Demonstrate HOT/WARM/COLD score calculation and transfer/callback route using synthetic lead.
6. (2 min) Demonstrate explicit DND; show suppression row and blocked subsequent dispatch.
7. (2 min) Show webhook signature validation, duplicate call_id idempotency, and post-call JSON.
8. (1–2 min) Review 30-case test evidence, known limitations, security, and cost model.

Never claim a live call, test, recording, transfer, or webhook succeeded unless you have actual evidence from your environment.
